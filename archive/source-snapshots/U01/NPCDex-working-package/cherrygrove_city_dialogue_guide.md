# Cherrygrove City: every line of dialogue, and how to reach it

## How to read this

This guide lists every line of dialogue in Cherrygrove City and says, in plain words, when you see it.

Cherrygrove City has seven parts here: the town outside, the Poké Mart, the Pokémon Center (a ground floor and an upper floor) and three houses on the south side of town. The middle house is probably the Guide Gent's.

Each entry says **When** you see the line, then gives the exact words in a quote. A blank line inside a quote means the text moves on to a new box. Separate quotes are separate messages.

Names the game fills in are shown in square brackets: [Player] is your name. (The guide would also use [Rival], [Pokémon], [Item] and [Number], but no line in Cherrygrove City uses them.)

"Probably" means the detailed research judged it but could not prove it. "Not confirmed" means it is an open question.

The code in square brackets at the end of each entry, such as [CGC-DLG-067-02a], is the key for looking that line up in the detailed reference file.

## The story in order

Most of Cherrygrove City does not change as the story moves on. The houses, most people on the street and in the buildings, the signs and the hidden items stay the same all game. Only the scenes on the street follow one another in a fixed order, so they get letters here. Everything else has a "When" note.

- **A. First arrival.** When you walk into the east side of town, the Guide Gent walks you round: the Pokémon Center, the Mart, the way to Route 30 and the sea. He ends at his own house and gives you the Running Shoes. This plays once.
- **B. After the tour.** When you walk along the north-west part of town, the Guide Gent comes back out of breath and loads the Map Card onto your Pokégear. This plays once.
- **C. After the scene in the house on Route 30 (Mr. Pokémon's house).** A scene in that house sets up the next street scene. When you next walk onto the east edge of town, a boy, probably your rival, stops you and battles you. His Pokémon depends on your starter. This plays once. The same Route 30 scene also sends away the two counter ladies in the Pokémon Center and brings in two other people at the same spots.
- **D. After the boy's scene.** The street scenes are finished. A later scene in Elm's lab in New Bark Town notes that you met the boy.

Two other changes happen "later in the story": the second Mart clerk appears, and two people (one in the Mart, one in the Pokémon Center) change their words. Both depend on a story marker that several places set (the Route 30 house, Elm's lab, the Violet City Gym and Mart, and later places). Exactly when each change happens is not confirmed. A person by the Pokémon Center stairs probably leaves after something in the Violet City Gym.

The photographer on the street comes and goes by badge and day of the week, not by story.

## Not confirmed

- Who most people are. None of them is named in their own words. The boy is probably your rival, but the game lists him only as a passer-by called "Boy".
- What happens if you lose to the boy. The scene does not check the result.
- Whether the two street signs show the lines given here. The pairing is probably right but not proven.
- Several steps in the scenes could not be read in detail, such as the Running Shoes and Map Card gifts, your Pokémon's movements and the door opening. Their effects are described from the lines around them.
- Which copy of the hidden Nugget on the Route 29 border is the one that works.
- What the counter people in the Pokémon Center (both floors), the delivery people in the Mart and the person by the stairs actually say or do. That lives in the game's own shared code.
- Exactly when the second Mart clerk appears and when the two "later in the story" lines change, and whether the second clerk is really hidden at the start.
- Which badge brings the photographer, and that his days are Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
- Nothing was played. All of this comes from reading the game's data.
