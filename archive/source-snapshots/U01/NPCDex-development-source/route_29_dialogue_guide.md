# Route 29: every line of dialogue, and how to reach it

## How to read this

This guide lists every line of dialogue on Route 29 and says, in plain words, when you see it.

Route 29 is the road between New Bark Town in the east and Cherrygrove City in the west. It has two parts here: the route outside, and the small gate building at its north end, which leads on to Route 46.

Each entry says **When** you see the line, then gives the exact words in a quote. A blank line inside a quote means the text moves on to a new box. Separate quotes are separate messages.

Names the game fills in are shown in square brackets: [Player] is your name, [Rival] the name you give your rival, [Pokémon] a Pokémon's name, [Item] an item, [Number] a number. Your friend is probably Lyra if you play as a boy and Ethan if you play as a girl.

"Probably" means the detailed research judged it but could not prove it. "Not confirmed" means it is an open question.

The code in square brackets at the end of each entry, such as [R29-DLG-033-02a], is the key for looking that line up in the detailed reference file.

## The story in order

Most of Route 29 does not change as the story moves on. The people on the route and in the gate say the same words all game. Only two things depend on how far you are, and some depend on the clock or the calendar. So this guide uses no lettered stages. Each "When" note names the story step, the day, the time or your choice instead.

- **Early in the game.** You can walk the route freely, for example on your errand west to Cherrygrove City. There is no friend scene yet.
- **After a scene in Elm's lab, once.** A scene in Elm's lab in New Bark Town sets up a meeting. Which lab scene it is, is not confirmed. The next time you step onto the east end of the route, your friend and Marill come to meet you. Your friend shows you how to catch a Pokémon, gives you Poké Balls and Mr. Paint, says goodbye, and both walk off. It never repeats.
- **Any time, by time of day.** One person on the route says a different line in the daytime, at night and in the morning.
- **After you hold a certain badge, on one day of the week (probably Tuesday).** Tuscany, one of seven siblings, can be found on the route. Which badge counts is not confirmed. Tuscany gives a TwistedSpoon once. Later, probably once you have met all the siblings around the game, Tuscany gives your Pokémon a Shock Ribbon.
- **The gate at the north end.** The two people there say the same thing all game.

Not confirmed: which badge counts, how the ribbon's "one per week" limit resets, and exactly which hours each time of day covers.

### Tuscany (probably only on Tuesdays)
[R29-EVT-033-04] [R29-EVT-033-01] [R29-EVT-033-LS]

Tuscany stands in the middle of the route. Each time you walk onto the route, the game checks two things: you must hold a certain badge (which one is not confirmed), and it must be the right day of the week, probably Tuesday. If both are true, Tuscany is there; if not, Tuscany is hidden. The name and "Tuesday" come from Tuscany's own words.

What Tuscany says depends on the first Pokémon in your party that can still battle, on what you have done before, and on the day.

**When:** that Pokémon already has the Shock Ribbon (probably). This is checked first, so it wins over everything below.

> on your Pokémon...

[R29-DLG-033-04b]

> me again next week!

## Things that happen without words

These are shared steps the whole game uses. Their own wording is not part of Route 29 and is not quoted here.

- After you read either sign on the route, a shared step closes the sign. It has no text. [GLB-RTN-2000]
- Your friend's music starts in the scene at the east end. [GLB-RTN-2029]
- The Poké Balls and Mr. Paint from your friend, and Tuscany's TwistedSpoon, are handed over by the shared item-giving step, which shows the game's usual item-received message. [GLB-RTN-2033]
- If your Bag has no room for the TwistedSpoon, the game's usual full-Bag message is shown and nothing is given. [GLB-RTN-2009]
- Picking up the Potion on the ground uses the game's usual pick-up step and messages. [GLB-RTN-ITEMBALL]
- Finding the Nugget uses the game's usual hidden-item step and messages. [GLB-RTN-HIDDENITEM]

## Unused or unreachable

Every message in Route 29's own text is shown by something. (Tuscany's lines live in a text file shared with the other siblings elsewhere in the game; only Tuscany's eight lines are covered here.)

- The gate has a spare action that does nothing and that nothing uses. [R29-EVT-134-01]
- The gate's list of things to do when you walk in is empty. It does nothing.
- In the scene at the east end, one copy of the "Was I too fast?" check can never run. [R29-EVT-033-02]

## Not confirmed

- Whether the boy and girl versions of each friend line are the right way round. This was read from the game's data, not tested in play.
- Who most speakers are. The people on the route and in the gate have no names. Tuscany is named only in Tuscany's own lines; the friend is named only in the text.
- The catching demonstration and the Marill's movements during the scene could not be fully read. They are described only from the lines around them.
- Three figures on the route have no lines and nothing says what they are. Two of them look the same.
- Which sign shows which words. The two sign texts are probably the right way round, but this is not proven.
- Whether the second copy of the Mr. Paint gift in the scene at the east end was added by the hack or was already in the game. The Mr. Paint item has no name in the original game's item list.
- Tuscany's lines come from a shared text file that is probably, but not certainly, the right one. Which badge Tuscany needs, whether the day is really Tuesday, and how the ribbon's "one per week" limit resets are also not confirmed.
- Which clock hours count as morning, daytime and night for the person waiting for Pokémon.
- Whether the Nugget near the west end can be found, since its spot sits on the edge of Cherrygrove City's part of the map.
- Nothing was played. All of this comes from reading the game's data.
