# New Bark Town: every line of dialogue, and how to reach it

## How to read this

This guide lists every line of dialogue in New Bark Town and says, in plain words, when you see it.

New Bark Town is the town outside plus five buildings, seven indoor rooms in all: Elm's lab; your house downstairs, with Mom; your room upstairs, where a new game starts; a small house with a woman and a child; another small house with one person; and a two-storey house on the street, where your friend is found in the room upstairs. The short code after each building name (such as T20R0101) is the game's own name for that room.

Each entry says **When** you see the line, then gives the exact words in a quote. A blank line inside a quote means the text moves on to a new box. Separate quotes are separate messages.

Names the game fills in are shown in square brackets: [Player] is your name, [Rival] the name you give your rival, [Pokémon] a Pokémon's name, [Item] an item, [Number] a number. Your friend is Lyra if you play as a boy and Ethan if you play as a girl.

"Probably" means the detailed research judged it but could not prove it. "Not confirmed" means it is an open question.

The code in square brackets at the end of each entry, such as [NBT-DLG-061-07a], is the key for looking that line up in the detailed reference files.

## The story in order

The town's people say different things as the story moves on. This guide splits the story into ten stages, A to J, and every "When" note below names one. The game fixes the order within each story step, but they do not prove the order between some later steps, so stages G to J are "probably" in this order.

- **Stage A: new game, before Mom's welcome.** You wake up in your house. Mom greets you and talks about your Trainer Card, saving and the options.
- **Stage B: after Mom's welcome, before you first enter Elm's lab.** You step outside. Your friend and Marill meet you and you are sent towards the lab.
- **Stage C: in Elm's lab, before you choose your first Pokémon.** Elm talks to you and you cannot leave the lab yet.
- **Stage D: you have your first Pokémon, but not the Pokégear.** You pick and name your Pokémon at the machine. The aide stops you at the door and gives you items. Outside, your friend congratulates you.
- **Stage E: you have the Pokégear and are on Elm's errand.** Mom gives you the Pokégear. If you try to leave west before that, you are stopped. When you do leave, Elm comes out and gives you his phone number.
- **Stage F: back from the errand.** On your return to the lab, an officer and your friend are there. You give your rival a name and you are handed the Egg. This stage lasts until the Violet City events.
- **Stage G: probably after the Violet City Gym** and related Violet City events. The game only shows that these come from Violet City; which exact event is not confirmed.
- **Stage H: probably after Dragon's Den.** A second lab scene plays with your friend and Elm. Mom, and later your friend, wait at the east exit of town. The aide also tells you a stolen Pokémon was returned.
- **Stage I: probably after the Bell Tower or Whirl Islands visits.** Elm's talk changes again.
- **Stage J: after you beat the Pokémon League.** Mom has a Hall of Fame scene. Elm gives you the S.S. Ticket, and later has optional talks.

Not confirmed: who or what marks the end of the main game, whether stage I or stage J comes first, and exactly where in the story the Mom and friend scenes at the east exit fall.

## Not confirmed

- **Who is speaking, for some people.** The game's character pictures could not be read with the tools used, so several speakers (people in the houses, the aide, some townspeople) are judged from what they say. Those are marked "probably". [NBT-EVT-060-01] [NBT-EVT-060-02] [NBT-EVT-062-01] [NBT-EVT-065-01] [NBT-EVT-066-01] [NBT-EVT-384-01]
- **Which version is for a boy and which for a girl.** Where a line has two versions (usually one naming Lyra and one naming Ethan), the guide says which player sees which. That comes from how the command is described, not from playing the game.
- **Cameron's lines.** That his words come from the shared photographer set was judged by matching the wording.
- **New box or scroll.** The game has two kinds of break between pages of text. This guide shows both as a blank line.
- **A few unexplained steps.** Some steps in the scenes (one at the west exit, some sign steps, a game-version check, the result of naming your rival) could not be decoded. What they do is guessed from what comes before and after. [NBT-EVT-060-03] [NBT-EVT-060-04] [NBT-EVT-061-01] [NBT-EVT-061-03] [NBT-EVT-061-12]
- **The hack's added gifts in Elm's lab.** The "all key items" and Porygon gifts run on the hack's added code. Their wording was read from that code, not from a built game. [GLB-RTN-2080] [GLB-RTN-2081] [NBT-EVT-061-04]
- **Exactly when some scenes happen.** Several scenes depend on events outside town (Violet City, Dragon's Den, Bell Tower, Whirl Islands, the League). The guide places them by the name of the place that causes them. If two scenes could start at once when you enter a map, which one wins was not checked.
- **Nothing here was played.** Everything comes from reading the game's data. "Can happen" means the data allows it, not that it was seen in play.
